# Posting copy — "UNREAD"

## Caption

> We invented writing so we'd be understood. These five didn't make it.

Short, no hashtag stuffing in the line itself, no question mark. It states a premise and lets
the video answer it. Avoid anything that reads as an ad ("learn a language today!") — the
whole design of this edit is that nobody knows it's an ad until 0:27.

Hashtags, on their own line: `#history #archaeology #ancienthistory #linguistics #voynich
#unsolvedmysteries`

Do **not** tag the app in the caption. The end card at 0:30 carries the wordmark and
`verbavia.com` for 1.35 seconds, which is the only call to action in the piece. Naming it up
top throws the reveal away and tells the algorithm to serve this to people who already know
you. Put the link in the bio; people will screenshot the last frame either way.

## Pinned comment

> Linear A: we can pronounce it correctly. We still have no idea what it says.

This is the strongest single fact in the video and the one people will not believe. It sends
them back to card 3 — a rewatch — and the replies argue about how that's even possible. (It
works because Linear B, its descendant, was deciphered in 1952, so the sign values carry over
but the language behind them doesn't.)

Second choice, if you want volume over depth:

> Which one would you want solved?

## Cover frame

Use the Phaistos Disc spiral at **~0:05.5** (frame 165) — it fills the frame, it's high
contrast, and it's an object people half-recognise without being able to name. It reads at
thumbnail size, which the Voynich text columns do not.

Do not use the first frame as the cover. The naked figures in the green pools are the right
opening *in motion*, but as a static thumbnail they're ambiguous in a way that costs you the
click.

## Posting notes

- **Sound on matters.** The edit is built around impacts and a drone. If a viewer watches
  muted, the type still carries the whole story — that was deliberate — but the drop at 0:03
  is doing a lot of work.
- **First comment within a minute.** Pin it immediately; the Linear A line is the retention
  driver in the comments.
- **Don't boost this as a paid ad without settling the CC BY-SA question** in
  `ATTRIBUTION.md`. Organic posting is a different risk profile than paid placement.

## If it performs

The format is a series. Same skeleton, same 80 BPM grid, swap the subject:

- **"Golden Record"** — the greetings in 55 languages currently 15 billion miles from Earth
  on a disc that will outlive the sun. All NASA public-domain footage, so zero licence risk.
- **"1977"** — fifty deaf children in a Nicaraguan schoolroom with no shared language who
  built a complete grammatical one from nothing. The only time in recorded history anyone has
  watched a language being born.

Both end on the same two-second turn. The account becomes "the channel about language you
didn't know you wanted," and the app is the thing at the end of every one.
