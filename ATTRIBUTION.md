## What this means for a commercial post

- **Public domain / CC0** — use freely, no credit required.
- **CC BY** — commercial use is fine; you must credit the author.
- **CC BY-SA** — commercial use is fine and you must credit, but share-alike can be read as
  requiring the derivative work to carry the same licence. For a brand ad that is a real
  consideration, not a formality.

The Linear A and Indus plates are the CC BY-SA ones. Two clean ways out if you'd rather not
carry that licence:

1. Put the credits in the caption or a pinned comment (what most creators do), or
2. Swap those two plates. Museum open-access programmes (the Met and the Cleveland Museum of
   Art both release CC0) hold comparable objects, and 19th-century excavation plates of both
   scripts are public domain by age.

Everything else — the Voynich, the Phaistos Disc, the Easter Island plate — is already public
domain or CC0.
